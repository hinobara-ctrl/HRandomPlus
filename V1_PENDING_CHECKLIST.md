These contracts already have implementations and regressions in the worktree. Recheck them after the pending changes rather than adding another design.

- [x] Native stable copying atomically owns a unique destination and never deletes another process's file.
- [x] Desktop generation atomically creates a unique `.osu` and keeps `BeatmapID: 0`.
